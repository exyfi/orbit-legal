Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Orbit keeps your data on your iPhone. The developer of Orbit runs no server for the app and receives none of the data described below.

What Orbit stores on your iPhone

  • The birth date, birth time and birthplace you enter.
  • Your lesson progress, XP and streak.
  • Your answers to reflection questions.
  • Your settings, such as the house system and whether notifications are on.

Orbit calculates your chart on the iPhone from the birth data each time it needs it. You can skip your own data and use the example chart.

What leaves your iPhone

Purchases. Apple processes Orbit+ subscriptions. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active, and sends the developer sales reports that do not name you.

Backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the backup can include Orbit's data. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud backups.

Orbit sends nothing else. It has no account, no analytics service, no advertising SDK and no tracking. The city search reads a list stored inside the app. Notifications are scheduled on the iPhone and need no server.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in Orbit and tap "Delete all my data". This removes your birth data, progress, reflection answers, settings and pending notifications. Deleting the app does the same.

An Orbit+ subscription lives in your Apple Account. Cancel it in the Settings app on your iPhone: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you the right to see, correct or erase personal data a company holds about you. The developer holds none of your Orbit data, so you exercise these rights on your own iPhone with the controls above.

Email

If you write to the support address, the developer receives your email address and your message, and uses them to answer you.

Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text appears on this page with a new date. A change in what leaves your iPhone will also appear in the app's update notes.
